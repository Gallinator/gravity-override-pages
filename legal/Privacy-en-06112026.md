The Controller processes the Users' Data for the following purposes:

The legal basis is Art. 6 letter E) of the GDPR, as the processing is necessary for the pursuit of a legitimate interest of the Controller or, if not applicable, the legal basis is Art. 6 letter B) as the processing is necessary to perform the obligations undertaken by the Controller through the User's acceptance of the APP's Terms of Use;

The collected personal data are kept for a period not exceeding the achievement of the purposes for which they are processed. In particular:

- Data collected for the purposes in point (b) are kept for the time necessary to handle the request and, in any case, no longer than 24 months from the handling of the request, except in the event of any litigation with the Data Subject;
- Data collected for the purposes in point (c) are kept for the time necessary to pursue the purpose and, in any case, after 24 months, they will be anonymized;
- Data collected for the purposes in points (d) and (e) are kept for the time necessary to pursue the purpose and, in any case, no longer than the ordinary terms of limitation and forfeiture.

Different data retention periods may be determined by specific legal regulations, orders from the Authority, or the Controller's participation in judicial proceedings involving data processing.
